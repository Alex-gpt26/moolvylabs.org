Moolvy Labs Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Moolvy Labs collects, uses, and protects the information you provide when using our website and products.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of data:

  • Personal information: your name, email address, and contact details you provide when registering, subscribing to our newsletter, or contacting support.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device information, and referrer (the page you came from).
  • Cookies and analytics: details about how you interact with the site, collected via cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • Providing stable operation, support, and improvement of our products and services.
  • Processing your requests, orders, and support inquiries.
  • Sending important notices, service messages, and security updates.
  • Analyzing site usage to optimize its structure and user experience (UX).

3. Sharing Data With Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except in the following cases:

  • With your explicit consent.
  • When required by third-party service providers solely to operate the Service.
  • To comply with lawful requests from government authorities and applicable law.

4. Data Protection and Storage

We apply modern technical and organizational security measures (including SSL/TLS encryption) to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data.
  • Opt out of receiving marketing communications at any time.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Policy as needed. All changes take effect once published on this page. We recommend checking this page periodically for updates.
